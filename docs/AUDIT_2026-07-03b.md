## Round 1 (static + copy agent; runtime covered by the live cycle above)

Tier 1: none.

Tier 2 (1, fixed):
1. A just-added Blue player could be silently misfiled to Yellow when the
   realtime refetch downloaded teams.json before this device's manifest
   upload landed (stale manifest -> orphan adoption to Yellow). FIX:
   local-manifest-wins merge before the orphan sweep, guarded so deleted
   players cannot resurrect and unmodified refetches do not re-upload.

Tier 3 (5, fixed):
- saveLocal wrote the old team's roster to the new team's key for one
  render on switch. FIX: prevTeamRef skip.
- Team switch silently discarded an unsaved lineup order. FIX: confirm
  prompt when orderDirty.
- Late JSONP response after the 10s timeout threw a global
  ReferenceError. FIX: no-op callback left behind on timeout.
- Double-import guard was state-only. FIX: synchronous importingRef.
- Hardcoded realtime channel topic could collide if useRoster were ever
  mounted twice. FIX: per-team + random-suffix topic (cleanup removes by
  instance, unaffected).

Noted acceptable (documented): JSONP executes an apple.com script
(trusted, term encoded, results React-escaped); manifest writes are
last-write-wins whole-file (roster edits are rare, PIN-gated).
